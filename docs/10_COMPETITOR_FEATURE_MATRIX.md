**Competitive thesis v2:** To compete at Rp99k we must **not** include costly custom frame design; make 15-shot disposable+voice+15-second video+note truly usable, with transparent storage ceilings and managed setup. At Rp150k+ charge for frame/strip customization, larger capacity, enhanced experience. Partner self-service reduces design workload; direct managed adds service but restricts revision entitlements.
